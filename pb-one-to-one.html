The Reality

You are not looking for motivation. You are looking for a clear path to becoming the player you know you can be.

You already care about your performance. You turn up to team training, go to the gym and are willing to work. You probably save sessions online, ask other players what they are doing and try to fit extra work around an already busy week.

You may be pushing for more game time, trying to earn a county opportunity or simply frustrated that your physical level does not yet match your ambition.

The issue is not that you are unwilling to work. The issue is that more effort without the right direction can still leave you in the same place. Your team programme cannot account for everything you need as an individual. Copying what another player does does not tell you what you need to improve next.

You need a plan that brings your gym work, speed, conditioning, recovery and nutrition together around your goals, your schedule and the current stage of your season.

But…

What keeps happening when nothing changes?

  • You keep training, but you cannot clearly tell whether you are becoming stronger, faster or fitter.
  • Your team training changes from week to week, but your gym plan does not adapt with it.
  • You add more work because you are worried you are not doing enough, then arrive at matches with heavy legs.
  • You stop gym work completely when fixtures get busy and lose the qualities you worked to build.
  • You jump between programmes, exercises and advice without staying with one clear progression long enough to see what works.
  • You compare yourself with stronger, faster or fitter players, but have no objective plan for closing the gap.
  • Every missed selection, poor performance or recurring niggle adds to the feeling that you should be further ahead.
  • You know you have more potential. You just do not have a clear system for turning that potential into consistent performance.
The biggest cost is not one poor session. It is finishing another season knowing you worked hard, but still wondering whether you prepared as well as you could have.

A Different Approach

You do not need another generic PDF, random collection of workouts or programme copied from an intercounty player.

Your plan will not look the same every week. A heavy team-training week, a match week, an off-season block and a return from injury should not all be treated the same way. Together, we make the right work clear, monitor how you respond and adapt as the season changes.

Common Questions

FAQs

What is PB Performance 1:1 Coaching?
Individual online coaching for ambitious GAA players. Your strength, speed, conditioning, recovery and nutrition are brought together around your needs and schedule, supported through regular check-ins, adjustments, tracking and direct access to Paddy.
Who is it for?
Adult hurling, camogie and Gaelic football players who are willing to work but want more structure, individualisation and accountability. You do not need to be an intercounty player — you just need to care about improving.
How does online coaching work?
After your initial assessment, your programme is delivered through the coaching app with exercise demonstrations and progress tracking. You complete a weekly check-in, receive adjustments and can contact Paddy directly when you need support.
How often will I train?
That depends on your team schedule, goals and stage of the season. Your plan is designed around the time you realistically have. More sessions are not automatically better.
Will the gym work leave me tired for matches?
The programme is planned around team training and fixtures. Volume and intensity are managed so gym work supports rather than competes with your performance on the pitch.
What is the investment?
The investment and coaching commitment will be discussed after your application, once we have confirmed that coaching is the right fit for you and your situation.
What makes this different from a standard online programme?
A standard programme serves a broad type of player. This coaching is shaped around your actual training week, goals, feedback and response — and it changes as those circumstances change throughout the season.
What happens after I apply?
Paddy reviews your application personally and contacts you to arrange the next step. There is no obligation and no hard sell.
