Privacy policy

PB Performance Coaching ("we", "us") is run by Paddy Burke and provides online strength and conditioning coaching to GAA players. This policy explains what personal information we collect through pbperformancecoaching.com and how we use it. We are the data controller for that information.

What we collect and why

  • Coaching application form. When you apply for 1:1 coaching you are taken to a Google Form that asks for your name, contact details and some information about your training and goals. We use this to review your application and contact you about coaching. The form is provided by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Free guide sign-up. If you enter your name and email to receive the free training guide, we use them to send you the guide and occasional emails about coaching. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. The list is managed by Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.), subject to Intuit's privacy statement.
  • Coaching clients. If you become a client we hold the information needed to coach you: contact details, training history, test results, check-ins and messages. This is kept for the duration of coaching and for up to two years afterwards, then deleted unless we need to keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Legal basis

We process application and client information because it is necessary to provide the coaching you have asked for (contract). We send guide and marketing emails with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or contacting us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. It is shared only with the service providers named above (Google, Mailchimp) and the coaching app used to deliver your programme, each of which processes it on our behalf. Some of these providers store data outside the UK and EU under standard contractual safeguards.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, or to provide a copy of it to you. To do any of these, email us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. We remember in your browser's session storage whether you have closed the free-guide pop-up, so it does not reappear during your visit; this is deleted when you close the browser and is not sent to us.

The embedded YouTube video is provided by Google and may set cookies when it loads or plays, under Google's cookie policy. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which records the request but does not set cookies.

Terms of service

The service

PB Performance Coaching provides individual online strength and conditioning programmes, check-ins and coaching support. Applying through this website does not create a coaching agreement; coaching begins only once we have spoken, agreed a start date and you have made your first payment.

Commitment, payment and cancellation

  • Coaching starts with an initial 90-day commitment, after which it continues month to month.
  • Fees are agreed before you start and are payable in advance for each period.
  • After the initial 90 days you can cancel at any time with notice before your next payment is due. Payments already made for the current period are not refunded.
  • If we are unable to continue coaching you for any reason, you will be refunded for any period you have paid for but not received.

Your responsibilities

  • Tell us about any injury, illness or medical condition before you start and whenever it changes. If in doubt, check with your doctor before starting a new training programme.
  • Follow your programme sensibly and stop and tell us if something causes pain.
  • Keep your check-ins and communication honest; the coaching only works with accurate information.

Results

Player results and testimonials on this site are genuine and shared with the players' permission. They show what some athletes have achieved; they are not a promise that you will achieve the same. Your results depend on your starting point, consistency, team schedule and many other factors.

Liability

Training carries a risk of injury. We take care to make your programme appropriate for you, but you take part at your own risk and are responsible for training safely. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or anything else that cannot be limited by law.

Content

Programmes, videos and resources we provide are for your personal use and may not be shared or resold. Photographs on this site are used with the permission of the people shown or their rights holders.
