The Core Problem

Hard work will only take you so far

At the start, simply going to the gym and training harder may have helped you improve.

But once you reach a certain level, more work is not always the answer. You need to know what qualities you need to improve, what your team training already covers and how everything fits together across the season.

The problem isn't your work ethic. It's not having a plan you trust.

Common Questions

FAQs

How does online coaching work?
After your initial assessment, your programme is delivered through the coaching app with exercise demonstrations and progress tracking. You complete a weekly check-in, receive adjustments and can contact Paddy directly when you need support.
How many sessions will I complete?
That depends on your team schedule, goals and stage of the season. Your plan is built around the time you realistically have. More sessions are not automatically better.
Will the gym work leave me tired for matches?
Your programme is planned around team training and fixtures. Volume and intensity are managed so that your gym work supports rather than competes with your performance on the pitch.
How long does coaching last?
Meaningful physical development takes time. Coaching begins with an initial 90-day commitment, after which we review the next phase around your goals and season.
Is this only for intercounty players?
No. The coaching is for ambitious GAA players from club level through to intercounty. Your current level matters less than your willingness to work, communicate and improve.
What happens after I apply?
Paddy will review your application personally and contact you to arrange a conversation. You'll discuss where you are now, what you want to improve and whether the coaching is the right fit for you.
