I created PB Performance Coaching because GAA players are expected to perform at an increasingly high level inside an amateur sport. Clubs do their best, but no team programme can fully account for every player's individual needs, schedule, injury history and goals.

I believe every player must take responsibility for their own preparation. My job is to give you the plan, coaching, feedback and education to do that properly — not sessions that look impressive on paper, but preparation that makes a genuine difference on the pitch.

Is This Right for You?

Be honest with yourself.

This is for you if…

  • You are an ambitious hurling, camogie or Gaelic football player who wants to reach a higher level.

Common Questions

FAQs

What is PB Performance 1:1 Coaching?
Individual online coaching for ambitious GAA players. Your strength, speed, conditioning, recovery and nutrition support are brought together around your needs and schedule, then supported through regular check-ins, adjustments, tracking and direct access to Paddy.
Who is it for?
Adult hurling, camogie and Gaelic football players who are willing to work but want more structure, individualisation and accountability. You do not need to be an intercounty player, but you do need to care about improving.
How does online coaching work?
After your initial assessment, your programme is delivered through the coaching app with exercise demonstrations and progress tracking. You complete a weekly check-in, receive adjustments and can contact Paddy directly when you need support.
How long is the commitment?
An initial 90-day commitment is required. This gives enough time to properly assess where you are, build real progress and see meaningful results. After 90 days, we review and decide together what the next phase looks like.
How often will I train?
That depends on your current team schedule, goals and stage of the season. Your plan is designed around the time you realistically have. More sessions are not automatically better.
Will the gym work leave me tired for matches?
The programme is planned around team training and fixtures. Volume and intensity are managed so gym work supports rather than competes with your performance on the pitch. This is one of the most important things we get right from the start.
What is the investment?
The investment and coaching details will be explained after your application, once we have discussed your situation and confirmed that coaching is the right fit for you.
What makes this different from a generic online programme?
A generic plan serves a broad type of player. This coaching is shaped around your actual training week, goals, feedback and response — and it changes as those circumstances change throughout the season.
What happens after I apply?
Paddy will review your application personally and contact you within 24 hours to arrange a free call. There is no obligation and no hard sell.
